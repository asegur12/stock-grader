# Stock Grader

## Problem

A new stock investor wants to know which stocks to invest in. They want to see
which stocks are doing well, why, and whether they are predicted to rise or fall,
so they can make informed choices.

## Initial requirements

- **R1** The top of the web app lists the 10 most valuable companies by market value, ranked, with the price of one share.
- **R2** Each of the 10 companies shows a graph of its current value and past price history.
- **R3** Clicking a company shows its current status as of today.
- **R4** Clicking a company shows relevant news that could affect its stock value.
- **R5** Each company shows a prediction, as a percentage, of how much its value may rise or fall.
- **R6** The interface is black and white, with green for rises, red for falls, and yellow only for estimates.
- **R7** Each company lists 2 relevant sources, shown when the user hovers over it.
- **R8** A single rising stock is shown in a separate grid below the main one.
- **R9** The prediction is based on past price data and comes with a disclaimer that it is only an estimate.
- **R10** The app shows the past week's stock values, with each change labeled Rise or Fall.
- **R11** The app cites the sources of its stock data and news.
